## What is ICSR?

Internal Control over Sustainability Reporting (ICSR) refers to the system of governance, processes, and controls designed to provide reasonable assurance that sustainability-related information is complete, consistent, traceable, and prepared in accordance with applicable reporting frameworks.

ICSR is formally articulated by the Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission (COSO) in its March 2023 publication *Achieving Effective Internal Control over Sustainability Reporting*. That guidance applies COSO’s 2013 Internal Control—Integrated Framework to sustainability and ESG reporting.

---

## How ICSR fits into this repository

This repository does **not** restate or summarize COSO guidance. Instead, it explores the **data, system, and architectural implications** of implementing ICSR in practice.

COSO’s guidance defines *what* effective internal control over sustainability reporting should achieve. The questions in this repository focus on *how those outcomes become feasible* when sustainability data is:

- non-financial and heterogeneous
- frequently estimated or modeled
- sourced from third parties across the value chain
- reused across multiple reporting and assurance regimes

---

## ICSR and assurance

ICSR underpins multiple sustainability assurance approaches, including (but not limited to):

- ISSA 5000 (IAASB)
- AA1000 Assurance Standards
- ISO 14064 / 14067 family
- AICPA attestation standards

Rather than being optimized for any single assurance standard, ICSR provides a **common control foundation** that allows sustainability information to be evaluated under different assurance lenses.

---

## Why ICSR matters now

Regulatory regimes such as CSRD/ESRS, ISSB standards, and California climate disclosure laws increasingly require organizations to explain:

- how sustainability information is governed
- how risks and assumptions are assessed
- how controls operate over sustainability data
- how consistency and comparability are maintained over time

ICSR is the control framework that makes those explanations credible.

---

## How to use the ICSR lens in this project

Questions in this repository may be associated with one or more COSO internal control components:

- Control Environment
- Risk Assessment
- Control Activities
- Information & Communication
- Monitoring Activities

These associations are intended to **support discussion**, not impose compliance requirements.

---

## What this project does not do

- It does not prescribe specific control procedures
- It does not define “minimum compliance”
- It does not replace COSO, regulatory, or assurance standards

Its purpose is to surface the **hard questions** that must be addressed for ICSR to be achievable at scale.
