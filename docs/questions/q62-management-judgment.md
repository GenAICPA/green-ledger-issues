### The Question

How should management judgments, overrides, and estimates be documented so they are visible, reviewable, and auditable without discouraging legitimate judgment?

### Why this matters

Judgment is unavoidable in sustainability reporting. Poor documentation creates governance and assurance risk; over-deterrence can distort reporting.

- COSO ICSR guidance
- ISSA 5000 (professional skepticism)
- COSO guidance on bias
