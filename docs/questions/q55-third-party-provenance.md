### The Question

What minimum provenance, documentation, and metadata are required for third-party sustainability data (e.g., suppliers, utilities, estimators) to support assurance?

### Why this matters

Sustainability reporting relies heavily on third-party data, especially across value chains. Without clear provenance, it is difficult to assess reliability, bias, or fitness for purpose.

### Catalyst sources

- ESRS E1 – Value chain data
- COSO ICSR guidance
- ISO 27001 / 27037 (evidence and provenance concepts)
- AICPA Trust Services Criteria
