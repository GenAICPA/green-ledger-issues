### The Question

How should organizational boundaries, consolidation approaches, and equity-share rules be expressed so they can be applied consistently by systems?

### Why this matters

Sustainability boundaries often differ from financial consolidation boundaries. Ambiguity undermines consistency and auditability.

### Catalyst sources

- GHG Protocol – Corporate Accounting Standard
- ESRS E1 – Scope and boundaries
- IFRS 10 / IFRS 12 (contrast)
- ISO 14064-1
