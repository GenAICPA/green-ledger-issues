# How should methodological changes over time be captured and made auditable?\n\n### Why this matters

Comparability over time is a core objective of sustainability reporting, yet methodologies frequently change due to evolving standards, improved data, or supplier coverage. Without explicit tracking, trend analysis may be misleading.

### Catalyst sources

- COSO ICSR guidance (change management emphasis)
- ESRS 1 – Consistency and comparability
- GHG Protocol – Corporate Accounting Standard
- IAS 8 (Accounting policy changes, as an analogy)
