### The Question

How should dual materiality assessments be documented, versioned, and connected to downstream data collection and reporting decisions?

### Why this matters

Under CSRD, dual materiality is iterative rather than static. Without versioning, it becomes difficult to explain why data was included or excluded at a given point in time.
