### The Question

How should uncertainty, estimation ranges, and confidence intervals be represented as structured data rather than narrative disclosures?

### Why this matters

Many sustainability metrics—particularly Scope 3 emissions, scenario analysis outputs, and transition risks—are inherently uncertain. Treating uncertainty solely as narrative obscures its impact on decision-making, comparability, and assurance.

If uncertainty is not represented as data, it cannot be tested, monitored, or consistently reused across reporting regimes.
